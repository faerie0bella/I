Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Inkie may process when you interact with her through Discord.

1. Information Inkie May Process

Depending on the features enabled, Inkie may process information such as Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, usernames, and information provided through commands.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used only to operate Inkie's features, respond to commands, provide requested functionality, and maintain the bot.

3. Information Sharing

Inkie's information is not sold or shared for advertising purposes. Information may be processed by services required to operate Inkie.

4. Data Storage

Information is stored only when necessary for a feature of Inkie. The types of information stored may change as new features are added.

5. Data Requests

If you have questions about information associated with your use of Inkie, contact the owner through The Fable Society Discord server.

6. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Inkie's features or data practices change.

7. Discord

Inkie operates through Discord. Your use of Discord is also subject to Discord's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
